Earned Trust in AI Code · Concept card

Review the evidence, not the lines.

The AI turns an agent's PR into claims, tries to break each one with checks anyone can re-run, and scores only the evidence. Where a promise to users changes, it refuses to decide. A named owner signs.

01

Claim

Each change becomes short claims, each tied to a requirement ID.

02

Attack

Fails on old code, passes on new. Undo one part. Old vs new on the same inputs. Proof.

03

Score

A fixed formula, 0–100, from the evidence only. The model's own confidence counts for nothing.

04

Gate

Touches warnings, generated code or public API, and no old input proves it? Needs an owner.

05

Owner signs

Named from git history. Signs what was checked, what was not, and the risk accepted.

How it balances

Principles

  • RiskDepth follows risk. Light checks on high scores; the owner's full attention on gated claims. A high score never clears the gate.
  • HallucinationIf it can't be re-run, it doesn't count. The agent's reasons are claims to test, not facts.
  • TransparencyEvery number links to the command that made it and the requirement it serves.
  • ConfidenceEarned from evidence by a published formula, never stated by the model.
  • QualityReadability measured before and after: copy-paste blocks, paths per function.
Who owns the decision
  • The AI advises with evidence. It never approves.
  • The owner (most commits on those paths) decides on every gated claim and signs a receipt.
  • Owners edit the review rules; every edit carries their name.
What you give up

Trade-offs

  • + The owner reads claims and evidence, not the whole diff.
  • + Weak evidence is shown as weak. Nothing is rounded up.
  • − Real builds cost time: the old-vs-new test run takes ~4 min.
  • − The gate is strict. At first many claims go to a human.
When not to trust it

Limits

  • Evidence is only as good as the inputs. Lines no old input runs stay unproven, so they go to a human.
  • Owner by git history is a guess: the most commits is not always the right person.
  • Shown on 3 of 4 sample branches. No production data yet.

How trust is measured

Measurement

  • EscapesReverts and incidents per area must not rise as review time falls.
  • Real findingsShare of AI findings the owner accepts.
  • RoutedShare of claims sent to a human, and time to merge.
  • Track recordTrust is earned per area and withdrawn after an escape.
